**🥾 What to Bring**

Footwear: Trainers or walking shoes you don’t mind getting a bit muddy. No heels, no regrets.

Clothes: Layers. British weather is famously indecisive.

Water: Stay hydrated — even if you're planning on replacing all fluids with lager later.

Optional: Snacks, sunnies, raincoat (aka the full British survival kit)

**🍻 Food & Drinks After**

Once we’ve had our fill of nature, we'll decamp to a local Hampstead pub (likely The Duke of Hamilton or The Flask, both historic and very atmospheric).

We'll grab some proper pub grub (and possibly a Sunday roast if we’re feeling fancy) and continue doing what we do best: chatting about AI, data lakes, and that one time someone accidentally emailed an entire customer database to themselves.

**⚠️ Precautions & Disclaimers**

Fitness level: You should be comfortable with gentle walking for a couple of hours. This isn’t Ironman, but also not a 30-second dash to the bar.

Terrain: Some paths may be uneven or muddy. Expect hills (nothing Everest-like, promise).

Dogs: Very welcome if you have one. 🐶 Please keep them leashed at all times.

Weather: If the forecast is truly dire (think Ark-building levels of rain), we may revert to Plan B (which involves a pub, obviously).

Health: This is an informal, at-your-own-risk ramble. Wear good footwear, be sensible, don’t duel with squirrels. If you feel unwell, stay home, and we’ll toast to your speedy return.

Getting lost: We do our best to keep at a cohesive pace so the group stays together. Find a buddy and follow the group closely. You've got this!
